Scope

This ADR authorizes recovery and repair of the existing authenticated Advisor OS
productive Home, canonical Smart Widgets, Cartera information hierarchy,
governed manual Policy entry, Advisor Commissions presentation, and governed
manual Activity flow for ADVISOR_OS_PRODUCTIVE_RECOVERY_001.

Recovery before rewrite is mandatory. Existing canonical mounts, writers,
read models, evidence owners, metric owners, session boundaries, and productive
Supabase authorities must be reused where they satisfy the required contract.
Parallel productive Homes and duplicate Policy or Activity writers are
forbidden.

This ADR prospectively expands and partially supersedes the execution
restrictions of ADR-019 and ADR-020 only for the scoped modules and phase named
above. ADR-019 and ADR-020 remain unchanged as historical and architectural
authority. Their canonical Forge Shell, Home boundary, responsive identity,
human-authority, and legacy-runtime separation continue to apply.

Authorized Surfaces

- scoped Home, Smart Widget, Cartera, Policy entry, Commissions, and Activity UI;
- canonical Forge Shell mounting and route registration when source evidence
  proves mutation is necessary;
- scoped app.js mutation when source evidence proves it is the canonical mount;
- read models, view models, adapters, and owner-preserving projections;
- existing Policy and Activity productive persistence integration;
- Timeline and official Activity metric consumption through their owners;
- additive, versioned, non-destructive, tenant-aware schema migration only when
  an evidenced gap cannot be satisfied by the existing authority;
- non-weakening RLS repair only when required by an evidenced productive gap;
- scoped tests, CI, browser evidence, Pages acceptance, Build Tree evidence,
  working-branch commits, push, and pull-request preparation.

Truth and Authority Boundaries

- Evidence Ownership and source validity remain governed by ADR-001.
- Official metric ownership remains governed by ADR-002 and ADR-014.
- Human decision authority remains governed by ADR-003 and Article 0.
- Missing facts remain unknown under ADR-004.
- Product Truth remains outside this phase and protected by ADR-005.
- Policy Truth remains owned by Policy Intelligence under ADR-006.
- Forecast Truth remains outside this phase and protected by ADR-007.
- Economic presentation remains evidence-bound under ADR-008.
- NBA remains voluntary, explained, and non-executing under ADR-009.
- NASH may explain but may not invent or execute under ADR-010.
- Relationship context remains non-manipulative under ADR-011.
- Mick may interpret observable activity but may not infer human worth,
  discipline, motivation, or character under ADR-013.
- Advisor Experience remains capability-building and non-dependent under
  ADR-016 and ADR-016A.
- Compensation presentation remains governed by ADR-017 and ADR-018; this phase
  does not authorize compensation calculation or Rule Pack changes.
- Quotes and Product boundaries ratified by ADR-021 and ADR-022 remain protected.

Policy Entry Boundary

Manual advisor entry is provenance-bearing evidence, not automatic official
carrier evidence. Missing documentary verification must remain provisional or
incomplete. A successful UI state requires authenticated, tenant-scoped,
read-after-write verified persistence through the canonical Policy authority.

Activity Boundary

Manual Activity entry must require human confirmation, actor and tenant scope,
source provenance, and deterministic idempotency. Persisted Activity may feed
Timeline and official derived metrics only through their owners. It must not
execute the next action, create calendar events automatically, infer human
judgment, or mount ADDLIFE runtime.

Compensation Boundary

The presentation may distinguish estimated, projected, earned, confirmed,
paid, pending, stale, conflicting, and unknown states. It may not create payout
truth, modify calculation engines, rewrite Rule Packs, or present estimates as
paid income.

Prohibited Surfaces

- direct mutation or automatic merge of main;
- force push, destructive migration, user-work deletion, or production-data
  deletion;
- weakened RLS, exposed secrets, cross-tenant access, or unverified frontend
  success;
- legacy ADDLIFE runtime mounting or new imports from quarantined runtime;
- parallel Home, duplicate Policy writer, or duplicate Activity writer;
- retrospective rewrite of existing ADRs or Constitution;
- Product Truth, Quote Truth, Forecast Truth, compensation calculations, or
  compensation Rule Pack mutation;
- Manager OS, human ranking, punishment, promotion, selection, or RODI mutation;
- automatic CRM action, task creation, calendar creation, or next-action
  execution;
- referral-domain mutation.

Validation and Closure

Implementation requires owner-specific unit and master tests, authenticated
runtime proof, real persistence proof where persistence is claimed, tenant and
RLS isolation, idempotency, Timeline projection, session logout scrub,
late-result rejection, responsive browser acceptance, protected-route
regression, Pages acceptance, and evidence that no legacy runtime or duplicate
writer is mounted.

Mocks, fixtures, or screenshots alone cannot prove productive persistence.
The phase must stop at a pushed working branch and review-ready pull request.
Human merge remains required.
