Decision

UI-M05D replaces the rejected single-product-biased result composition with a
coherent Material 3 multi-product decision workspace for Imagina Ser, Vida
Mujer, SeguBeca and ORVI. Product accents are presentation-only. Floating
ForgeShell navigation remains canonical; temporary overlap is accepted only
when every result and action remains recoverable through normal scrolling.

Boundaries

Calculations, Product Truth, Product Intelligence, product rules, parsers,
mappers, rate cache, accepted-quote contracts, PDF extraction, Supabase, Nash,
ForgeShell and Home remain protected.

Owner color-depth annex

The owner rejected the first UI-M05D color treatment because product identity
and semantic hierarchy remained too subtle. Controlled product color depth is
authorized without changing layout or data behavior. Product accents must
appear across the identity, executive hero and supporting surfaces; verified,
missing and conflicted states retain explicit text or symbol labels. Owner
visual acceptance remains pending after the rework.
