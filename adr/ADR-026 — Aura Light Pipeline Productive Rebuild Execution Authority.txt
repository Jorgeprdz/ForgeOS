Decision

Authorize the productive visual and interaction rebuild of the canonical authenticated Pipeline in Forge Aura Light 2026.

The new Pipeline must use the existing productive prospect, Timeline, Stage RPC, source filter, stage filter, journal, administration, Calendar draft-handoff, NASH and NBA authorities. This scope does not authorize a duplicate data service, duplicate Stage writer, alternate Pipeline application, demo records or a parallel source of truth.

This is a real module redesign. It is not a scaffold, placeholder, CSS skin over fake content or Home dashboard work.

Required Experience

- One productive Pipeline surface with two views of the same records: Cards and List.
- Shared search, filters, count, selected Stage and productive data across both views.
- View preference may persist locally because it contains no private prospect data.
- Cards retain the useful prospect content already implemented but are rebuilt visually in Aura Light.
- List presents a compact operational row with Prospect, Stage, Source, Last activity, Next commitment and Actions.
- The visible action group is limited to WhatsApp, Call, Timeline and More.
- More opens an accessible contextual menu containing secondary actions such as Calendar, NASH Combat, NBA, Edit and Archive when each authority is available.
- Destructive actions appear separated and use the danger semantic.
- Icon artwork may be visually small, but every interactive target must remain at least 44 by 44 CSS pixels.
- Mobile may collapse the List layout into compact cards without losing actions or context.

Protected Productive Contracts

- Stage changes continue through the existing confirmed RPC authority.
- A successful Stage mutation requires a confirmed returned prospect row.
- Existing card or row DOM identity must not be replaced during the Stage save interaction.
- No authentication refresh may be triggered by Stage save.
- Source cannot be mutated as a side effect of Stage change.
- Filters must continue reconciling against the same productive cards.
- WhatsApp remains manual navigation; no automatic message send is authorized.
- Calendar remains a reviewed Google Calendar draft handoff; opening it is not a confirmed saved event.
- Timeline and journal remain the source of verified activity history.
- Archive must retain history and must not be represented as a hard delete.
- Anonymous users remain fail-closed from private records.

Validation

Required evidence includes:

- productive cards and list render the same records;
- view switching does not reload or lose filters;
- view preference persistence contains no private data;
- source, Stage and name filters remain functional;
- Stage mutation keeps its confirmed RPC and in-place behavior;
- WhatsApp, Call and Timeline quick actions remain correctly labelled;
- More menu supports keyboard, Escape, click-away and focus return;
- secondary and destructive menu actions call their existing authorities;
- missing phone states are honest and disabled;
- loading, empty, filtered-empty, error and anonymous states are explicit;
- mobile, tablet and desktop reflow;
- zoom and keyboard acceptance;
- no Material 3 tokens, components or visible palette;
- no Home mutation;
- no mutation outside the authorized scope.
