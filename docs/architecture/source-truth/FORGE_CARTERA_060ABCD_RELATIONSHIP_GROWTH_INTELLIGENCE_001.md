# FORGE CARTERA 060A–060D — RELATIONSHIP GROWTH INTELLIGENCE 001

## Goal

Detect natural, evidence-backed review possibilities inside existing relationships without treating people as inventory.

## Internal gates

- `060A`: second-policy, protection-review, referral-relationship and center-of-influence candidates.
- `060B`: evidence, consent, explicit willingness and non-manipulation boundaries.
- `060C`: governed advisor review before any Pipeline opportunity can exist.
- `060D`: productive read-only surface in Cartera.

## Ownership

Cartera owns the advisor-facing review surface. It does not own canonical identity, Relationship Graph truth, Pipeline opportunity lifecycle, Conservation formulas, final NBA priority, NASH final messages or contact execution.

## Non-manipulation lock

A life event is never a sales trigger. Referral and center-of-influence candidates require explicit client-confirmed willingness. A candidate is not an opportunity, lead, task, message or contact instruction.

## External effects

All automatic opportunity creation, contact execution, referral requests, final-message generation and final priority truth remain blocked.
