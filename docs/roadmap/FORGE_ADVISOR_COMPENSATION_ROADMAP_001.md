# FORGE ADVISOR COMPENSATION ROADMAP 001

Status: ACTIVE ROADMAP / CONTROLLED IMPLEMENTATION

## Purpose

Build the productive Advisor Compensation domain without confusing quoted premium, issued premium, paid premium, earned commission or paid commission.

ForgeOS must reuse the useful legacy calculator assets while replacing its IndexedDB authority, silent defaults and UI-owned calculations with versioned rules, confirmed economic events, append-only compensation events and evidence-backed period snapshots.

## Scope

Included:

- direct advisor initial commission;
- direct advisor renewal commission;
- Life and GMM commission interpretation;
- Training Allowance and direct advisor bonuses after rule validation;
- potential, estimated, earned, paid, adjusted, reversed and conflicting states;
- monthly advisor income snapshots;
- Commissions UI connection;
- Income Smart Widget connection;
- payout evidence reconciliation.

Excluded:

- Partner compensation;
- Manager compensation;
- advisor-development compensation generated by connecting or developing other advisors;
- team compensation;
- automatic payout confirmation;
- product recommendations optimized by commission.

## Constitutional Chain

```text
Policy Truth
-> Payment Obligation
-> Payment Evidence
-> Human Confirmation
-> Confirmed Payment Event
-> Compensation Rule Snapshot
-> Advisor Compensation Event
-> Advisor Compensation Period Snapshot
-> Commissions UI
-> Income Smart Widget
```

A confirmed premium payment may support earned commission. It does not prove that the carrier paid the commission. Paid commission requires separate payout evidence and human confirmation.
