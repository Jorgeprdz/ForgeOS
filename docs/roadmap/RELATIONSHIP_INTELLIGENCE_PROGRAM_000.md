# RELATIONSHIP INTELLIGENCE PROGRAM 000

## Purpose

Build seven productive capabilities on top of the accepted CRS 00–11 Commercial Relationship Spine without creating duplicate person, relationship, timeline, scoring, forecast, reminder, reporting, or action authorities.

## Product outcomes

1. Nash recommends the next best commercial movement.
2. Forecast estimates likely commercial outcomes using explainable evidence.
3. Cooling alerts identify relationships losing momentum.
4. Funnel reports describe real movement, conversion and blockage.
5. Relationship scoring summarizes observable commercial behavior.
6. Pre-meeting briefs assemble the relevant relationship context.
7. Intelligent reminders propose follow-up timing from accepted history.

## Shared authority

All seven capabilities consume one shared, read-only Relationship Intelligence Envelope derived from:

- CARTERA_010B commercial person authority;
- CRS_01 advisor commercial relationship;
- CRS_08 unified person Timeline;
- CRS_09 productive person workspace;
- CRS_10 accepted relationship intelligence composition;
- CRS_11 end-to-end relationship acceptance;
- canonical Pipeline, Activity, Quote, Application and Policy authorities.

The program must not create a second person, relationship, Timeline, score ledger, forecast ledger, task ledger, reporting truth store or autonomous business-action engine.

## Program stages

### RIN-000 — Scope, authority and safety lock

- Inventory existing Nash, Forecast, reporting, scoring, reminders and brief assets.
- Select canonical reusable engines and retire duplicate interpretations.
- Define evidence, explanation, confidence and human-control contracts.
- Lock read-only operation and no-effect boundaries.

### RIN-010 — Relationship Intelligence Envelope

Create one deterministic, advisor-scoped read model containing:

- identity and relationship references;
- current commercial state;
- chronological evidence;
- recency and cadence signals;
- commitments and overdue commitments;
- opportunity, quote, application and policy state;
- source availability and degradation;
- data freshness;
- evidence lineage and digest.

Unknown, unavailable and zero must remain distinct.

### RIN-020 — Cooling and momentum signals

Produce explainable states such as:

- ACTIVE;
- WARM;
- COOLING;
- COLD;
- BLOCKED;
- WAITING_ON_CLIENT;
- WAITING_ON_ADVISOR;
- COMPLETED;
- INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE.

Signals must be based on accepted dates, commitments, movement cadence and domain state. No opaque personality judgment.

### RIN-030 — Relationship score

Create an explainable score made from visible subdimensions:

- engagement;
- recency;
- commitment fulfillment;
- commercial progression;
- responsiveness;
- relationship depth;
- data confidence.

The score is advisory context, not human worth, financial capacity, insurability or automatic eligibility.

### RIN-040 — Nash next best movement

Nash consumes the shared envelope, cooling state and score to recommend:

- the next movement;
- why it is recommended;
- supporting evidence;
- urgency;
- confidence;
- what information is missing;
- safe alternatives.

Nash may draft but must not automatically contact, message, create tasks, schedule, advance Pipeline, create opportunities, applications or policies.

### RIN-050 — Commercial forecast

Build explainable probability and timing projections for accepted outcomes, including:

- next meaningful interaction;
- quote progression;
- application likelihood;
- policy issuance likelihood;
- expected timing range;
- risk of delay or loss;
- confidence and evidence coverage.

Forecast is not accounting truth, commission truth or guaranteed production.

### RIN-060 — Intelligent reminders

Generate reminder proposals from:

- explicit commitments;
- overdue follow-ups;
- cooling thresholds;
- scheduled meetings;
- quote/application/policy milestones;
- recommended next movement.

Creation, scheduling, messaging and Calendar actions always require explicit human confirmation.

### RIN-070 — Pre-meeting brief

Compose a concise brief containing:

- who the person is;
- relationship history;
- last interactions;
- open commitments;
- current opportunities and products;
- objections and concerns supported by evidence;
- next best movement;
- risks, gaps and questions to ask.

The brief must cite source evidence and expose stale or unavailable sources.

### RIN-080 — Funnel and relationship reports

Produce advisor-scoped reporting from canonical events and states:

- people entering each stage;
- movement between stages;
- conversion and elapsed time;
- stalled and cooling relationships;
- quotes, applications and policies by lineage;
- forecast versus actual outcome;
- Nash recommendation acceptance and outcome;
- reminder effectiveness.

Reports must not derive truth from UI state or duplicate transactional records.

### RIN-090 — Productive workspace and Smart Widgets

Connect the accepted read models to:

- Home Smart Widgets;
- Person workspace;
- Pipeline contextual surfaces;
- Activity and Reports;
- Forecast;
- Nash Combat;
- meeting preparation surfaces.

Responsive acceptance is required for mobile, tablet and desktop, preserving the floating mobile navigation safe zone.

### RIN-100 — End-to-end productive acceptance

Validate one deterministic relationship journey across all seven capabilities, including degraded sources, late-result rejection, logout scrub, evidence lineage, explanation, no-effect boundaries and human-confirmation gates.

## Delivery order

The build order is:

1. RIN-000 authority lock;
2. RIN-010 shared envelope;
3. RIN-020 cooling signals;
4. RIN-030 score;
5. RIN-040 Nash recommendation;
6. RIN-050 Forecast;
7. RIN-060 reminders;
8. RIN-070 pre-meeting brief;
9. RIN-080 reports;
10. RIN-090 productive UI and Smart Widgets;
11. RIN-100 integrated acceptance.

Cooling and scoring come before Nash and Forecast because both require stable, explainable signals. Reminders and briefs consume the same recommendation and evidence contracts. Reports are connected only after the underlying authorities are accepted.
