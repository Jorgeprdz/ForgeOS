## Phase questions answered

### 1. How much productive intelligence exists?

At least 48 significant capability-level assets were evidenced across the current architecture, in addition to a historical root scan of 219 filename-selected candidates. The 48 are the normalized set used for assembly planning; the 219 are not treated as canonical authorities.

### 2. How much is really exposed?

18 capabilities meet the strict GREEN criterion. 22 are productive/valid but materially underused (YELLOW). Therefore the central product problem is activation/composition, not a shortage of engines.

### 3. What valuable capabilities are dormant?

15 high-value dormant/underused capabilities are recorded, with the top ten listed above.

### 4. What is duplicated?

12 duplicate/convergence groups are recorded. Most are generation/role overlaps rather than legitimate duplicate truth authorities.

### 5. Where is Aura reinterpreting meaning?

Recent Aura phases already removed or prohibited several local interpretations. Remaining risk is concentrated in generic product aliases, productivity KPI interpretation, forecast generation selection, priority/ranking lineage and economic scenario presentation.

### 6. Which domains have clear authority?

Commercial Person/Timeline, Policy/Coverage, Product Truth, confirmed payment economic evidence, governed compensation calculation, Activity/FES, and the recommendation-vs-human-action boundary are comparatively clear.

### 7. Which domains require Phase 2 reconciliation?

Cross-generation FIP/Material3/Aura composition, Forecast, cross-domain decision projection, advisor lifecycle/compensation coupling, and Pipeline↔Cartera identity continuity.

### 8. Top ten underused assets?

Recorded above and in the Dormant Intelligence Register.

### 9. Main fourth-rebuild risks?

Reimplementing FIP intelligence inside Aura; allowing Home or individual workspaces to own business truth; treating projections as truth; letting Prospect/Person identity diverge; and choosing legacy/current engine generations ad hoc.

### 10. Ready for assembly blueprint?

Yes. Evidence is sufficient to begin a documentation-only authority/composition blueprint. The next phase must not implement integrations yet.
