## Completeness contract

`AUTHORITATIVE_PACKET_KEYS` remain available through the immutable runtime snapshot. `VISIBLE_UI_KEYS` are selected from product-specific structured models; sections are not flattened into a single string. `INTENTIONALLY_HIDDEN_INTERNAL_KEYS` are implementation identifiers and raw governance diagnostics. Missing advisor-facing values are classified as `NOT_PRESENT_IN_AUTHORITY`, `INTERNAL_ONLY`, `NOT_APPLICABLE`, or `BLOCKED_BY_UNKNOWN_EVIDENCE`; no known supported-product field is allowed to remain an `IMPLEMENTATION_DEFECT`.

The presentation layer does not mutate calculators, Product Intelligence, parsers, mappers, rates, or product rules.
