# ADVISOR COMPENSATION SCOPE AUTHORITY LOCK 001

Status: LOCKED / STAGE 000

## Decision

ForgeOS authorizes a dedicated direct Advisor Compensation domain.

This domain may interpret direct advisor commission and direct advisor bonus truth. It must not absorb Partner, Manager, team or Advisor Development compensation.

## Authorized Scope

```text
ADVISOR_COMPENSATION
```

## Explicitly Excluded Scopes

```text
PARTNER_COMPENSATION
MANAGER_COMPENSATION
ADVISOR_DEVELOPMENT_COMPENSATION
TEAM_COMPENSATION
```

Advisor Development compensation includes economic outcomes generated by connecting, recruiting, supervising or developing other advisors. Those outcomes require a separate authority and may not be silently added to direct advisor policy commission.

## Locked Truth States

```text
UNKNOWN
POTENTIAL
ESTIMATED
EARNED
PAID
ADJUSTED
REVERSED
BLOCKED
CONFLICTING
```

### UNKNOWN

Required evidence or rules are absent. Unknown is not zero.

### POTENTIAL

A future economic scenario exists, but the required payment or eligibility condition has not occurred.

### ESTIMATED

A rule-backed calculation exists, but it is not yet eligible for earned or paid truth.

### EARNED

A valid policy, product identity, confirmed premium payment, applicable rule snapshot and reproducible calculation exist.

### PAID

The compensation itself has carrier payout evidence and human confirmation. A paid premium is not proof of paid commission.

### ADJUSTED

A later append-only event corrects an active compensation event.

### REVERSED

A later append-only event reverses an active compensation event.

### BLOCKED

A forbidden scope, shortcut or unsupported use was attempted.

### CONFLICTING

Evidence, rules, periods, products, currencies or amounts disagree and require reconciliation.

## Locked Economic Distinctions

```text
QUOTE != ISSUED_POLICY
ISSUED_POLICY != PAID_PREMIUM
PAID_PREMIUM != EARNED_COMMISSION_UNLESS_RULES_APPLY
EARNED_COMMISSION != PAID_COMMISSION
SIMULATION != EARNED_COMMISSION
SIMULATION != PAID_COMMISSION
```

## Forbidden Shortcuts

```text
ISSUED_PREMIUM_AS_PAID
QUOTE_AS_INCOME
DEFAULT_COMMISSION_RATE
UNKNOWN_PRODUCT_CALCULATION
AUTOMATIC_PAYOUT_CONFIRMATION
PRODUCT_RECOMMENDATION_BY_COMMISSION
```

### Issued Premium as Paid

Policy issuance cannot create paid-premium truth.

### Quote as Income

A quote, proposed premium, Pipeline opportunity or forecast cannot create compensation truth.

### Default Commission Rate

An unknown product, variant, period or policy year may not fall back to a generic commission rate.

### Unknown Product Calculation

Unresolved product identity must remain blocked or unknown.

### Automatic Payout Confirmation

A calculated or earned commission may not become paid without payout evidence and human confirmation.

### Product Recommendation by Commission

Compensation information may not optimize or bias product recommendations.

## Authorized Uses

```text
BUILD_POTENTIAL
CALCULATE_ESTIMATE
PROMOTE_EARNED
PROMOTE_PAID
APPLY_ADJUSTMENT
APPLY_REVERSAL
BUILD_PERIOD_SNAPSHOT
RUN_SIMULATION
```

Unknown uses are blocked rather than silently permitted.

## Promotion Gates

### Potential

May be created from a valid policy/product scenario with clearly labeled future conditions.

### Estimated

Requires policy truth, product truth and an applicable compensation rule snapshot.

### Earned

Requires:

```text
POLICY_TRUTH
PRODUCT_TRUTH
CONFIRMED_PAYMENT_EVENT
COMPENSATION_RULE_SNAPSHOT
REPRODUCIBLE_CALCULATION
```

### Paid

Requires all earned prerequisites plus:

```text
COMPENSATION_PAYOUT_EVIDENCE
HUMAN_PAYOUT_CONFIRMATION
```

### Adjustment or Reversal

Requires a prior active compensation event. Historical events are not overwritten.

## Mutation Boundary

The Stage 000 boundary evaluator is read-only. It classifies authority, missing evidence and forbidden operations. It does not calculate money, write compensation events, mutate Cartera, mutate Policy Truth, promote payout state or alter UI.
